2. Privacy & Data Protection

This section describes how Patient Data and User data are handled. The Clinic is the Data Fiduciary (controller) for Patient Data it enters; the software publisher acts as a Data Processor on the Clinic's instructions.

2.1 What is collected

  • Patient personal data: name, age, sex, phone, address, MRN, allergies.
  • Patient health data (sensitive): vitals, diagnoses, clinical notes, prescriptions, investigations, follow-up dates, photo/PDF prescription attachments.
  • Pharmacy / commercial: sale records, bill numbers, payment modes, stock and batch information.
  • User account data: name, username, hashed password, role, phone.
  • Operational logs: minimal request logs for security and debugging.

2.2 Lawful basis (DPDP Act 2023)

  • Consent obtained by the Clinic from the patient for the purpose of providing them medical care.
  • Legitimate uses recognised under Section 7 of DPDP, where applicable (e.g. medical emergency).
